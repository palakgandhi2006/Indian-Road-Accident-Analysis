# 🚦 Indian Road Accident Analysis | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

Indian Road Accident Analysis is a Data Analytics and Business Intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI.

The objective of this project is to analyze road accident patterns across India, understand accident severity, identify high-risk time periods and locations, and explore environmental factors associated with road accidents.

The project transforms raw accident data into interactive dashboards to uncover meaningful insights and support data-driven road safety decisions.

## 🎯 Business Problem

Road accidents are a major public safety concern. Understanding accident patterns, severity, casualties and geographical distribution can help identify areas and time periods that require greater attention.

This project explores the following business questions:

- How are road accidents distributed across different states?
- Which months and hours experience more accidents?
- How does accident severity vary?
- What patterns can be observed across weekdays and weekends?
- How are visibility and other available environmental factors associated with accidents?
- Which areas require greater road safety attention?

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall road accident trends across India.
- Understand accident severity and casualty distribution.
- Identify high-risk hours, days and months.
- Compare accident occurrences across states.
- Explore environmental factors associated with accidents.
- Present meaningful insights through interactive dashboards.

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

This project consists of four interactive dashboard pages.

### 1. Accident Overview Analysis

Provides an overview of road accident occurrences and their distribution.

Key Areas:
- Total accident records
- Total casualties
- Accident severity distribution
- State-wise accident analysis
- Overall accident patterns

### 2. Time & Location Analysis

Explores accident patterns across different time periods and geographical locations.

Key Areas:
- Accident patterns by hour
- Monthly accident trends
- Weekday and weekend comparison
- State and geographical analysis
- High-risk time periods

### 3. Road & Environmental Risk Analysis

Examines available environmental factors and their relationship with accident occurrences.

Key Areas:
- Visibility analysis
- Environmental condition patterns
- Accident distribution across available risk indicators
- Comparison of accident patterns under different conditions

### 4. Accident Severity & Impact Analysis

Focuses on accident severity and casualty patterns to understand the impact of road accidents.

Key Areas:
- Severity distribution
- Casualty analysis
- Monthly severity trends
- Comparison of accident severity categories
- Identification of areas requiring road safety attention

## 💡 Key Project Findings

- Analyzed 20,000 accident records.
- Identified 34,529 casualties across the recorded accidents.
- Minor accidents accounted for 55.1% of the records.
- Maharashtra recorded the highest number of accidents in the dataset.
- Examined accident patterns across time periods, locations and severity categories.

## 📈 Business Recommendations

1. **High-Risk Period Monitoring:** Focus road safety monitoring on time periods with higher accident occurrences.
2. **State-Level Safety Planning:** Prioritize road safety initiatives in states with higher accident counts.
3. **Visibility Awareness:** Improve awareness and safety measures during low-visibility conditions where supported by the data.
4. **Accident Prevention:** Strengthen road safety awareness and preventive measures.
5. **Data-Driven Decisions:** Use accident trends and severity patterns to support road safety planning.
